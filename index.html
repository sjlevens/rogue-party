Rogue Party

A dice adventure for iPhone, iPad and Android

Need help?

Open an issue on this site's issue page and describe what happened, which device you play on and the game's version (shown at the bottom of Settings). You need a free GitHub account to post.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. There is no account or sign-in. Uninstalling the game deletes your progress unless your device backs it up.
How do I remove ads?
Buy "Support the dev" in Settings. It is a one-time purchase that turns ads off for good.
I bought "Support the dev" but ads came back on a new device.
Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.
When do ads show?
Only after a run has ended, never during a run, and not on every run.
How do I change my privacy or ad choices?
In Settings, under Privacy choices (shown where the law requires it), and in your device's privacy settings.
